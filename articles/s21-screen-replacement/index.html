Samsung Galaxy S21 Screen Replacement Guide

Updated 2026-10-02·Screen Replacement & Repair·~780 words

Replacing a Samsung Galaxy S21 screen means fitting a new Dynamic AMOLED display with a built-in ultrasonic fingerprint sensor, which is a more delicate job than older Samsung models because the fingerprint reader and screen are tightly integrated. Genuine Samsung service parts and quality aftermarket panels are both available, but they aren't identical in performance.

Key takeaways

  • The S21 uses a Dynamic AMOLED 2X display with an in-screen ultrasonic fingerprint sensor, so the fingerprint reader's function depends on the replacement screen being compatible and correctly installed.
  • Samsung's official parts and repair program (including some self-repair options in certain regions) use genuine AMOLED panels, while aftermarket alternatives vary widely in colour accuracy and touch quality.
  • Curved-edge displays like the S21's are more prone to lifting or separating at the edges if adhesive isn't applied correctly during a repair.
  • The S21's screen is glued in rather than clipped, so replacement always involves removing old adhesive and applying fresh adhesive or pre-cut adhesive strips for a proper reseal.
  • An aftermarket screen can affect touch accuracy near the curved edges or produce a slightly different colour temperature compared with Samsung's original AMOLED panel.

What makes the S21 screen different to replace?

The Galaxy S21's Dynamic AMOLED 2X display integrates an ultrasonic in-display fingerprint sensor directly beneath the glass, rather than a separate physical sensor. This means the fingerprint reader's performance is tied to the specific screen assembly fitted — a poor-quality or incompatible replacement can make the fingerprint sensor less reliable or stop it working altogether.

The S21's screen also uses a gentle curve at the edges (less pronounced than the S21 Ultra's but still present on some variants), which adds complexity to achieving a flush, well-sealed fit during reassembly.

OEM vs aftermarket screens for the S21

Genuine Samsung AMOLED service parts most closely replicate the phone's original colour accuracy, brightness, and fingerprint sensor compatibility. Quality OEM-grade aftermarket panels can come close but may show minor differences in colour temperature or peak brightness. Budget aftermarket screens are more likely to have inconsistent touch response, a dimmer maximum brightness, or fingerprint sensor issues, since replicating an AMOLED panel with an embedded ultrasonic sensor accurately is technically demanding.

It's reasonable to ask a repairer directly which tier of part they use and whether the in-screen fingerprint sensor is tested as working before the phone is returned to you.

Why adhesive and sealing matter on this model

Samsung phones from this era rely on adhesive, not clips, to hold the screen to the frame, and that adhesive also contributes to the phone's water resistance rating. A rushed repair that reuses old adhesive or doesn't apply a full new adhesive seal can leave the edges of the screen prone to lifting over time and reduce the phone's resistance to dust and moisture. This is worth asking about, particularly around the curved edges where a poor seal is more likely to show.

What affects the cost of an S21 screen replacement

Cost is influenced by the part tier (genuine Samsung AMOLED vs OEM-grade vs budget aftermarket), whether the fingerprint sensor function is preserved and tested, and labour complexity tied to the curved-edge adhesive reseal. Because pricing varies between repairers and part availability shifts over time, it's worth checking a current, itemised price list for your exact model rather than relying on a number seen elsewhere.

DIY vs professional repair

Replacing an S21 screen at home is possible but considerably more fiddly than on older Samsung models, mainly due to the in-screen fingerprint sensor and curved adhesive seal. Mistakes can mean a non-functional fingerprint reader or a screen that doesn't sit flush at the edges. Given the phone's value and the technical sensitivity of the fingerprint sensor, most owners are better served having this repair done professionally, with fingerprint function tested before the phone is returned.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Galaxy S21 fingerprint sensor still work after a screen replacement?

It should, provided a compatible screen assembly is used and the repair is done correctly, but lower-quality aftermarket screens can make the in-display ultrasonic fingerprint sensor less reliable or non-functional.

How much does it cost to replace an S21 screen?

Cost depends on the part tier used (genuine Samsung, OEM-grade, or budget aftermarket) and labour, and varies between repairers — check a current, itemised price list for an accurate figure for your model.

Is the Samsung S21 screen replacement waterproof afterward?

Water resistance depends on a proper fresh adhesive seal being applied during the repair; reused or poorly applied adhesive can reduce the phone's original resistance to dust and moisture.

Can I replace my Galaxy S21 screen myself?

It's possible but more technically demanding than older Samsung models because of the embedded fingerprint sensor and curved-edge adhesive seal, so many owners choose a professional repair to avoid fingerprint or sealing issues.
